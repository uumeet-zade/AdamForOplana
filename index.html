The Legislative Agenda

Our 4 Core Priorities

Grounded in the Social Democratic Alliance manifesto, our campaign puts forward four decisive legislative measures to guarantee economic justice, quality education, and community dignity in Oplana.

Support The Plan Support The Plan →
01 Housing & Urban Equity

More Affordable Housing

Safe, affordable housing is a fundamental human right. We will dismantle bureaucratic bottlenecks and stand up against predatory corporate landlords speculating on Oplana homes.

  • The Caprica Foundations Act: Compounding tax incentives and streamlined permits for builders of non-profit and cooperative housing.
  • Strict Rent Stabilization: Establishing tough caps on speculative rent increases across all residential districts in Oplana.
  • Public Land for Public Good: Prioritizing municipal and regional land for high-efficiency communal housing cooperatives.
Caprican Senate Pledge:

Deliver 3,000 new energy-efficient, rent-controlled homes in Oplana within our first legislative term.

02 Fair Taxation & Economy

Higher Inheritance Tax on the Ultra-Rich

Caprica's current fiscal system is unfairly regressive. Billionaire dynasties pass on untaxed fortunes while working taxpayers shoulder public burdens. We will restore true economic fairness.

  • Dynasty Fortune Tax: Introducing higher marginal tax brackets exclusively targeting the top 1% multi-millionaire inheritances and trusts.
  • Closing Evasion Loopholes: Eliminating offshore shell maneuvers and establishing strict transparency on mega-estate transfers.
  • Direct Reallocation: Ring-fencing 100% of collected ultra-rich estate revenue to fund Oplana’s schools, public transit, and emergency care.
Caprican Senate Pledge:

Zero tax hikes on middle- or lower-income families; extreme inherited fortunes must contribute to national renewal.

03 Education & Youth

More Investments in Education

Our children and youth are the future of Caprica. We will modernize classrooms, expand technical apprenticeships, and ensure no young person in Oplana is priced out of their potential.

  • Classroom Modernization: Direct capital funds to renovate aging schools, equip science & technology labs, and lower student-to-teacher ratios.
  • The START Academy: Free weekend and post-secondary educational programs for high schoolers and university students in diplomacy, public service, and tech.
  • Debt-Free Vocational Pathways: Fully paid apprenticeships and living stipends for students entering high-demand skilled sectors.
Caprican Senate Pledge:

Establish a specialized Cooperative Research & Technology Center in Oplana linked directly with local secondary schools.

04 Families & Labor Rights

Supporting Families & Workers

A strong society starts with strong, supported families. We will establish historic labor protections, accessible childcare, and proactive community care so that every parent has peace of mind.

  • Comprehensive Social Care Network: Catching families before hardship strikes through universal family counseling, mental health access, and youth clubs.
  • Historic Labor Protections: 50-hour workweek cap, 35 paid sick days (21 days at 100% pay), and 90% salary coverage for long-term leave.
  • Free Emergency Healthcare: 100% free prehospital ambulance care nationwide—no family should fear a bill during a medical crisis.
Caprican Senate Pledge:

Expand free early childhood education spots and guarantee universal after-school care for working Oplana parents.
